# AMAZON SALES & PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

## 1. Business Background

Amazon operates in a competitive e-commerce industry where understanding sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, and order management is essential for business growth.

Sales data provides valuable information about revenue generation, product performance, monthly sales trends, and customer orders. However, without proper analysis, it can be difficult to identify which areas of the business are performing well and where improvements are needed.

This project uses SQL to analyze Amazon sales data and generate meaningful insights that can support informed business decisions.

## 2. Business Problem Statement

The business needs a clear understanding of its overall sales performance and order activities. It is important to determine how much revenue is generated, how many orders are recorded, which product categories contribute the most revenue, and which months experience the highest sales.

Additionally, the business needs to evaluate order statuses to understand the distribution of completed, pending, and cancelled orders.

Without these insights, identifying sales opportunities, monitoring business performance, and improving order management may be challenging.

Therefore, this project will use SQL to examine Amazon sales data, identify important business trends, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve sales performance and operational efficiency.

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate the overall sales revenue generated by the business.
- Determine the total number of orders recorded.
- Identify the highest revenue-generating product categories.
- Analyze monthly sales performance and identify the best-performing month.
- Evaluate completed, pending, and cancelled orders.

## 4. Business Questions

The analysis will answer the following five business questions:

1. What is the total sales revenue generated?
2. How many orders were recorded?
3. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
4. Which month recorded the highest sales?
5. How many orders are completed, pending, and cancelled?

## 5. Expected Business Value

The project is expected to provide insights that will help the business:

- Understand its overall revenue performance.
- Identify the most profitable product categories.
- Recognize monthly sales trends.
- Monitor order completion and cancellation rates.
- Make informed decisions about sales improvement and order management.

## 6. Tools and Technologies

- **MySQL Workbench:** SQL query development and execution.
- **SQL:** Data analysis, filtering, aggregation, and sorting.
- **Microsoft Word:** Project documentation and reporting.
- **GitHub:** Project storage and portfolio presentation.

## 7. Project Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw Amazon sales data into meaningful business insights. By analyzing revenue, orders, product categories, monthly performance, and order statuses, the project aims to help the business understand its performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
